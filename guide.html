1.2 Google Classroom

Google Classroom is where teachers post assignments, materials, and announcements for each class. You must be signed in with your BFS Google account (@bfs.or.kr) to access it.

How to log in to Google Classroom
  1. Go to classroom.google.com or open Google Classroom from your Google apps grid (the 9-dot icon in Gmail).
  2. Make sure you are signed in with your @bfs.or.kr account, not a personal Gmail. If you see a personal account, click your profile picture → Add another account.
  3. Your classes should appear automatically on the home screen once your teacher adds you.
  4. If a class is missing, ask your teacher for the class code and click the + button → Join class.

Tip: Enable Google Classroom notifications so you don't miss assignment due dates. Go to the menu (☰) → Settings → Notifications.
